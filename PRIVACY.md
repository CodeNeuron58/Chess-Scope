# Privacy

Owlchess has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies**.

- **Your games stay on your device.** The chess engine runs in your browser. Your library, reviews,
  drills and settings are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage). Clearing the
  site's data, or Settings → Your data → Clear library, removes them.
- **What goes over the network — only when you ask for it:**
  - your chess.com or Lichess username, to fetch your public games from their official APIs;
  - a Lichess game link you paste, to download that game;
  - if you add a Lichess API token: the positions you open in the Explorer, sent to Lichess.
- **Engine files** (Stockfish) are downloaded from the site once and stored in your browser's
  Cache Storage; Settings → Your data → Downloaded engines shows their size and removes them.
- **Your Lichess token** is stored in your browser and sent only to Lichess.
- The site host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as any website does.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/CodeNeuron58/Owlchess/issues.
